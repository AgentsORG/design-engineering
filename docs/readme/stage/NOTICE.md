# Notices

The kit in `kit/`, the filming scripts in `scripts/` and the capture route in
`playground/` are from [Stageshow](https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills) by
Benji Taylor (MIT, HEAD `299140f`, Stageshow 1.1.1); see `LICENSE`. They are
trimmed to the parts the scenes import: `kit/index.ts` and the capture route
were rewritten for that, and nothing else in them was changed. The scenes in
`examples/de-*` and `examples/_de/` are adapted from Stageshow's
`hero-demo`, `static-card`, `ai-chat`, `ai-cards` and `raycast` examples;
`examples/pov-*` and `examples/_pov/` from its `overlap` example and its
bento rules.

Product names (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, OpenCode) appear as
plain text, as the harnesses the project installs into; they remain with their
owners and imply no endorsement. No third-party logos or artwork are bundled.

`examples/_de/claude.tsx` writes Claude Code's start header, turn marks and
input box as plain terminal text; no Anthropic artwork is drawn or bundled.

The taste stills (`examples/pov-*`) render one invented reminder card in the
looks that `skills/design-engineering/pov-examples/` describes for Duolingo,
Campus Binge, Smash&Clash, Swiggy, Cursor and Aave. They use only colour,
type and shape values written in those files and original copy; no logo,
mascot, character art or other brand artwork is drawn or bundled, and the
names appear as plain text only. The names and looks remain with their
owners and imply no endorsement. Their fonts (Fredoka, Nunito, Poppins,
Montserrat, Luckiest Guy, Lilita One, Rubik, Outfit, Geist, Geist Mono and
JetBrains Mono, all SIL OFL) load from Google Fonts at render time; Outfit,
Geist, JetBrains Mono and Fredoka stand in for Gilroy, CursorGothic, Berkeley
Mono and Feather Bold, which are not freely available.

No fonts, photographs or brand SVGs are bundled.
