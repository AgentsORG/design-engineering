# MOC — Anti-Patterns

The frame: *could this have been generated in 30 seconds with a prompt?* If yes, fix it.

This MOC is short on purpose — most anti-patterns are subtle enough to live as one consolidated reference. When in doubt, load this one.

## Nodes

- [[ai-default-tells]] — Purple gradients, Inter/SF Pro, bootstrap card layouts, lorem ipsum, pure black dark mode, default Lucide icons, gradient buttons without reason, hero + 3-cards + FAQ marketing pages. The full list.
- [[content-authenticity]] — Real data not lorem. Specific odd numbers ("127 customers", not "100+"). Written copy that sounds like a person. Real photos. Dates with context.

## Cross-cluster

- Specific defaults are addressed in their own MOC: [[color-monochromatic]] for color, [[typography-humanity]] for fonts, [[dark-mode]] for `#000`, [[icon-systems]] for Lucide.
